## Limits

These are directional results, not pass-rate estimates: L1 has three samples and L4 one per arm.
Prompts forbade commands and edits and restricted reads to the fixture and assigned rule, but tool
logs were not captured, so compliance was not independently verified. Harness agents also had loaded
rules; the current-arm L1 outputs went against the loaded old wording, reducing but not eliminating
contamination risk.
